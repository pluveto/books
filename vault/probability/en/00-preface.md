# Preface

Probability gives uncertainty a model you can compute with; statistics then uses that model to draw conclusions from data. This book covers the foundations of the former: events, probability and random variables.

## Structure of the book

- [[01-events]]: sample spaces, events and the axioms of probability.
- [[02-random-variables]]: random variables, expectation and variance.

> [!info] The calculus you need
> The expectation of a continuous random variable is an integral. [[01-limits]] and [[02-derivatives]] from *Calculus* are enough.

## Notation

$\P(A)$ is the probability of an event $A$; $\E[X]$ and $\Var(X)$ are the expectation and variance of a random variable $X$.
