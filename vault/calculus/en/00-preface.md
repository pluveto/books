# Preface

Calculus answers two questions: how fast a quantity is changing, and how large an accumulation of many tiny quantities becomes. Both answers rest on a single idea — the limit.

## Route

1. [[01-limits]]: turn "arbitrarily close" into inequalities you can check.
2. [[02-derivatives]]: define the instantaneous rate of change with a limit.

> [!note] Prerequisites
> You should be comfortable with real numbers, functions and inequalities. Linear algebra is not required, but [[01-vectors]] from *Linear Algebra* makes the multivariable parts easier.

## Conventions

Functions are real-valued and defined on an interval of $\R$ unless stated otherwise. Differentials are always written $\dd x$.
